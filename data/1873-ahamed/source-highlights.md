The real return that a typical investor in Britain was able to earn by investing in stocks went from 13 percent per year in the quarter century before 1873 to less than 5 percent per year in the quarter century afterward. Even in the United States, where the economy was stronger, real returns on stocks fell from 11 percent before 1873 to less than 7 percent after. In fact, the period from 1880 to 1896 has gone down as the longest bear market in U.S. history.
